How this was built

The forecast is driver-based: Shopify sales and refunds by SKU, fees, chargebacks, payout timing, Amazon settlements, wholesale terms by retailer, ad spend by platform, bills with terms and dates. Variance is split by swapping one driver at a time from forecast to actual, so the pieces add up to the line exactly. A finding needs to beat both the driver's normal noise (3 standard deviations) and Fernwood's materiality ($2,500). The draw, bill and paydown actions come from actions/plan.py and actions/vendor_rules.yaml. Accuracy is measured on hidden events in evals/results.
